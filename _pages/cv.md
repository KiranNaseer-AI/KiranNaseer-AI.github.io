## Research Profile

Doctoral researcher at the University of Gujrat with ten years of university teaching experience. My work asks when AI systems fail the people they were built to serve — the worst-off client in a federation, the hospital whose scanner differs from the rest, the domain a vision–language model was never fine-tuned on.

The PhD develops **FORGE** — a unified framework for diagnosing, benchmarking and stabilising AI under real-world distribution shift, spanning federated learning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, multimodal robustness and label-efficient medical image segmentation.
