I am a doctoral researcher at the **University of Gujrat, Pakistan**, working on a central question in applied AI: *when do AI systems fail the people they were built to serve?*

My PhD develops **FORGE** — a unified framework for diagnosing, benchmarking and stabilising AI under real-world distribution shift. FORGE spans four axes: federated robustness benchmarking, worst-client fairness in federated NLP, vision-language model instability under physical domain shift, and label-efficient medical image segmentation.

I bring ten years of university teaching experience under the Higher Education Department, Punjab, alongside an active international research programme.

## Research Interests

- Federated learning robustness under Non-IID distribution shift
- Worst-client fairness and evaluation in distributed ML
- Vision-language model instability under physical domain shift
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA, PEFT) for federated NLP
- Medical imaging AI — brain tumour segmentation, active learning
- Privacy-preserving machine learning
